# Teaser Photos

Put your couple photos here! They'll appear in the intro sequence forming **C ♥ I**.

## Instructions

1. Name your photos numerically: `1.jpg`, `2.jpg`, `3.jpg`, etc.
2. Supported formats: `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp` (it tries each in order)
3. Update `PHOTO_COUNT` in `src/App.jsx` to match how many photos you added
4. Square or near-square photos work best (they're displayed in circles)
5. Keep files under 200KB each for fast loading

## Minimum & Ideal

| Photos | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **0** | Glowing placeholder dots (still looks good) |
| **10** | Minimum — some photos repeat to fill 27 positions |
| **27+** | Ideal — every position gets a unique photo |

## Grid Layout

The photos form three shapes:
- **C** — 8 positions (left)
- **♥** — 10 positions (center)  
- **I** — 9 positions (right)

Total: **27 positions**
